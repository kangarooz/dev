## The three — 1:10

`[ACTION]` Scroll to the definitions.

`[SAY]` "Hybrid is running more than one retrieval approach and combining the results — you
keep control of the combination and you carry the wiring. Full-text retrieval pulls whole
documents rather than fragments, which matters when the answer needs surrounding context to
make sense. Unified is the managed one from episode nine: less wiring, less visibility."

`[HOLD]` 5s.

---

## Escalation signals — 2:10

`[ACTION]` Scroll to the escalation section. This is the heart of the episode.

`[SAY]` "What you want are symptoms you'd actually notice. Answers that are right about the
topic but miss the specific identifier — that's semantic alone straining, and a signal for
hybrid. Answers that are technically sourced but incoherent because the fragment was too
small — that's a signal for full-text."

`[SAY]` "Symptom first, then option. Never the other way around — do not pick the most
sophisticated retrieval you know and go looking for a justification."

`[CALLOUT]` Symptom first, option second

---

## The traps — 3:10

`[SAY]` "Every option here costs something. Hybrid costs wiring and gives you two sets of
results to reconcile. Full-text costs context window — pull whole documents and you can crowd
out the room your chat step needs to think. Unified costs visibility."

`[SAY]` "None of that is a reason to avoid them. It's what to weigh."
